Our story

Making everyday help easier to find

Helping.com is designed to make discovering local assistance simpler by bringing everyday services and nearby helpers into one platform — so a broken tap or a stalled bike doesn't have to mean an hour of phone calls.

Our mission

Connect people with the right nearby help, quickly and with confidence.

For people who need help

Compare distance, ratings and price in one place instead of guessing who to call.

For local helpers

A simple way to be found by people nearby who already need what you offer.
